2. Where the action bar sits

What it's for: holds Save, Test and Switch (and later buttons). What differs: only where the bar is and its shape. All three use explicit Save.

A — Docked bar along the bottom of the right pane (sidebar stays full height)

B — Floating rounded bar centered over the bottom of the content; the content scrolls under it

C — Full-width bar along the bottom of the whole window, under the sidebar too

3. How saving works

What it's for: knowing your edits are kept. What differs: whether you press Save, or every change is kept at once and the bar says so. Press "Make an edit" to see each one react.

Explicit — edits are pending until you press Save; Discard throws them away; switching profiles with unsaved edits asks first

Automatic — every edit is saved at once (what CEB does today), and the bar now says "All changes saved"

4. Test button

What it's for: trying a profile without committing to it. What differs: whether there is a Test button.

Test — switches now, then goes back to the screens you had after 15 seconds unless you press "Keep"

No Test — only Switch; use your shortcut to switch back
